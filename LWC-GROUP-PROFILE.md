## 1. About the Company

**LWC GROUP (Pty) Ltd** is a **multidisciplinary engineering and consulting company** based in
Pretoria, South Africa, serving clients across **South Africa, the Democratic Republic of Congo,
and the wider African market**. We work in **English and French**.

Founded by the **Lukwichi brothers** and registered in 2026, LWC Group brings **engineering,
artificial intelligence, metallurgy and digital expertise** together under one roof. Our name —
*King of Engineering Solutions* — reflects a simple promise: **we turn your data, your processes
and modern AI into results you can measure** — cost saved, recovery gained, decisions improved.

We work **evidence-first**: we prove the value in your own data before we build anything on it,
and we start small — so every step earns the next.
