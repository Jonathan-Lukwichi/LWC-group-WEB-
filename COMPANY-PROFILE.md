## 2. Who we are

> *"Two brothers. One sees the system, one sees the machine."*

LWC Group is built by **Jonathan** and **Christian Lukwichi**.
One works in **industrial systems, simulation, data analysis and machine learning**;
the other in **metallurgy, mineral processing and electromechanical systems**.
Between us we cover how a plant behaves *as a system* and how the equipment inside it

single-service shop: the same two-lens thinking (system + machine) extends naturally
across several engineering and digital disciplines.

## 3. Vision, Mission & Goals

**Vision (TO CONFIRM / refine).**
To become Africa's trusted multidisciplinary engineering-and-operations consultancy —
the partner industrial businesses call when a problem crosses disciplines.

**Mission.**
Turn breakdowns no one can master into reliability everyone can — by starting small,
proving the data first, and only building what the evidence justifies.

## 6. The Method (Maintenance & Reliability — Discipline A)

*"We start small, and prove the data before we build on it. Each stage tells you whether the next one is worth doing."*

| Stage | Name | Time / Basis | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Maintenance System Review | 1–2 days · no data required | Plant walk + structured scorecard; 2–3 immediate low-cost actions |
| **02** | Maintenance Data Diagnostic | 2–3 weeks · one data export | Real MTBF, worst assets by true cost, prevention-vs-failure map, honest go/no-go |
| **03** | Reliability Workbench | conditional on Stage 2 | AI-drafted FMEA, RCA, job plans → engineers review & approve |
| **04** | Condition & Prediction | conditional · deliberately narrow | Only the 2–3 worst rotating assets, using data already collected |

## 7. Trust — what we will *not* claim

- We will **not** promise reduced downtime before seeing your data.
- We will **not** recommend software, sensors or a platform in the first engagement.
- If the diagnostic shows your records cannot support further analysis, **we say so and stop** —
  rather than build on a foundation that will not hold.

**Where we use AI:** for the sorting work too slow to do by hand — reading tens of thousands of
work-order records, classifying them, drafting documents from your own history, finding fleet-wide patterns.
**Where we do not:** engineering decisions, and writing to control systems. Everything is advisory and offline.
